## Research Interest
- **AI for Autonomous Scientific Discovery and Mechanism Inference:** Integrating physics and materials science with advanced AI to enable autonomous scientific discovery. My work moves beyond correlation (e.g., CTGNN, VQCrystal) towards the mechanistic understanding of complex systems. I focus on building closed-loop hybrid architectures that learn, refine, and evolve knowledge, mimicking the human scientific process.
-**Constrained Large Language Models (LLMs) and Neuro-Symbolic Systems:** Developing robust methods to harness LLMs for high-level scientific reasoning and decision-making. My contributions include engineering knowledge-graph-enhanced RAG systems and LoRA fine-tuning for improved scientific query accuracy, while addressing the core limitations of unconstrained LLMs. I specialize in neuro-symbolic systems and the design of dual-layer language systems (AxiomDSL and AxiomIR) to enforce logical rigor and structural consistency in machine-generated hypotheses.
-**Energy Systems and Advanced Materials Engineering:** Applying AI and rigorous system design to accelerate the discovery and optimization of functional materials essential for energy and chemical processes. This includes experience in materials modeling and generation, as well as multi-mode system engineering demonstrated by the successful development of the CPV-TE hybrid cell system (increasing efficiency from 25% to 38%). I focus on integrating hard physical constraints and simulation-grounded verification to ensure that new material hypotheses are truly testable and physically valid.
